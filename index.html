Add to the battle

Initiative — leave it blank and a d20 + Dex mod is rolled for you. Click the number badge on a portrait to change it mid-fight.
HP — click the health bar and type -7 for damage, +4 for healing, 22 to set it exactly, or t8 to grant 8 temporary HP. Damage eats temp HP first, just like the rules. At 0 HP the portrait greys out but keeps its place in the order.
Conditions — click the dashed strip under any name to toggle prone, poisoned, concentrating and the rest. Type a number in the box beside one to give it a duration: the counter ticks down at the end of that character's turn and the condition falls off by itself when it reaches zero. Leave the box blank and it lasts until you remove it.
Rounds — the fight sits at Round 0 until you press Start Battle. Both reset buttons return you there.
Battle log — every hit, heal, condition, reorder and turn is recorded down the right side, newest first and grouped by round, so "who took damage five turns ago?" is a scroll rather than an argument. Click the heading to fold it away.
Undo — ↶ and ↷ in the turn bar step back and forward through anything you've done, log included (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). History is kept for this tab only, so reloading starts it over.
Full stats — hover a portrait and click ✎ for AC, temp HP, notes and everything else.
Settings — the ⚙ button in the corner holds text size, accent colour and the behaviour switches. Text size scales the whole tracker, portraits included, for reading it from across the table.
Icons — in Settings, This device uses whatever emoji your device draws. Universal downloads one shared set so the tracker looks the same on the laptop and the iPad; it needs the internet the first time you switch, then works offline.
Reorder — on a phone the portrait is the grip: drag it up or down to move that character in the order. Swiping anywhere else on the row scrolls the page.
Turns — Space or → advances, ← goes back. Everything saves automatically, so you can close the tab and pick the fight back up.
