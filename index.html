Add to the battle

Initiative — leave it blank and a d20 + Dex mod is rolled for you. Click the red number on a portrait to change it mid-fight.
HP — click the health bar and type -7 for damage, +4 for healing, 22 to set it exactly, or t8 to grant 8 temporary HP. Damage eats temp HP first, just like the rules. At 0 HP the portrait greys out but keeps its place in the order.
Conditions — click the dashed strip under any name to toggle prone, poisoned, concentrating and the rest. Type a number in the box beside one to give it a duration: the counter ticks down at the end of that character's turn and the condition falls off by itself when it reaches zero. Leave the box blank and it lasts until you remove it.
Rounds — the fight sits at Round 0 until you press Start Battle. Both reset buttons return you there.
Full stats — hover a portrait and click ✎ for AC, temp HP, notes and everything else.
Icons — System uses whatever emoji your device draws. Universal downloads one shared set so the tracker looks the same on the laptop and the iPad; it needs the internet the first time you switch, then works offline.
Turns — Space or → advances, ← goes back. Everything saves automatically, so you can close the tab and pick the fight back up.
