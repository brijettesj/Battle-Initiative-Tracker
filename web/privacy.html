Privacy Policy

Roll Initiative — A Dungeon Battle Tracker

Last updated 4 September 2026

The short version. Roll Initiative does not collect, transmit or store any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores

Roll Initiative saves your encounters so you can close it and pick a fight back up. That includes:

  • Names, initiative scores, armour class, hit points, conditions and notes for the characters and creatures you add
  • Any portrait image you choose or upload
  • The battle log for the current encounter
  • Your settings, such as text size and accent colour

All of it is written to storage on the device the app is running on. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and the developer cannot see any of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it permanently.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number or location
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting
  • It does not contain third-party advertising or tracking software
  • It does not share, sell or disclose anything to anyone, because it holds nothing to share

Network access

The app is built to work with no internet connection at all, and does not need one to run. There is exactly one optional exception.

In Settings, the Icons option can be switched from "This device" to "Universal", which draws every portrait from one shared emoji set so the app looks the same on every device. Choosing "Universal" downloads that font once from Google Fonts. That request tells Google's servers your device's IP address, as any web request would. It happens only if you choose that setting, the font is cached afterwards, and no information about you or your encounters is sent with it. Leaving the setting on "This device", which is the default, means the app makes no network requests whatsoever.

Children

Roll Initiative is a utility for tabletop roleplaying games and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. If you choose to set a custom portrait, your device will ask you to pick an image; that image is copied into the app's own storage on your device and goes nowhere else.

Changes to this policy
